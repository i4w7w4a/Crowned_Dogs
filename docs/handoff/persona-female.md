# Persona, female base (from Art's Tripo parts, 2026-10-27)

- `persona-female.glb` (2.4 MB): the game file. Body 30k + head 15k triangles, hair = 668 strips (16k triangles) from Art's strands over a 14k dark cap on the 61-bone UE skeleton, +Y up, faces +Z, feet at y = 0, skull top 1.70 m (1.75 m with hair). 1K WebP textures, meshopt. In the arena as `apps/web/models/persona-female.glb`.
- Clips: Art's five Tripo clips untouched, 24 fps: `fire` (1.5 s), `grenade_throw`, `hit_by_shot`, `pistol_fire`, `pistol_aim` (5 s each); plus `idle`, `walk`, `run` baked from three.js X Bot (../xbot/Xbot.glb) for the arena until adapted clips replace them.
- `fem.blend` (158 MB, textures packed): the assembled full-detail scene. Since 19:33 the hair is Art's strand groom (Fem_Hair_Strands.blend, 66,584 curves, 334 guides, his GN «guide deformation + viewport density» and melanin hair material), seated by `hair/seat.py` with the exact transform of the mesh hair (scale 0.289, root empty bone-parented to `head`): FemHairRoot, FemHairStrands, FemHairGuides (hidden in renders). The old Tripo mesh hair is kept hidden as FemHairMesh. FemHairCards and FemHairCap are the game hair (hidden in renders).
- `hair/`: `seat.py` (strands onto the head), `cards.py` (strips: one per guide, as wide as the 80th percentile of its strands across the strip, two layers, drawn-hair texture `hair-strands.png`, smooth shading; cap = mesh hair shrunk 6 mm), `export_fem.py` (game export with the strips), `hairmask.mjs` (sets the strip material to alpha MASK 0.15, double sided, before the gltf-transform steps). In three.js cut-out materials get `alphaToCoverage`. `strands-vs-strips.jpg`, `strips-in-browser.jpg`.
- `assemble.py`: `python -I assemble.py -- FemBody.fbx Femhead.fbx FemHari.fbx OUT.blend [S_B S_H up back scale]` with Blender 5.2 as a Python module (`pip install bpy==5.2.3`). Body 1.464x (keyed translations scaled too), head 0.245x seated on the neck with the body's neck top bent to the collar outline, hair fitted over the skull by ray casts (then +14 mm up, 0.975x), head skinned neck_01 to head, hair 100 % head.
- `export_game.py`: `python -I export_game.py -- fem.blend Xbot.glb raw.glb` (decimate 0.6/0.3/0.27, bake idle/walk/run, export), then `gltf-transform resample, dedup, prune, resize 1024, webp --quality 88, meshopt --level medium` (@gltf-transform/cli 4.5.1).
- `render.py`, `anims.py`: Cycles check renders (head views, clip contact sheet); `head-check.jpg`, `tripo-clips-check.jpg`.
- Viewer page (private): https://claude.ai/artifact/GeU1c51BY963iEvWFM1SYF
- Art's originals: the three zips he uploaded in the core thread (FemBody, Femhead, FemHari). Never modify them.
