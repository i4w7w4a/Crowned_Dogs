Финальный промпт:

> Redesign the EMPTY fitting room in reference 1 from scratch, matching the restrained cinematic near-photoreal cold neutral gray exposure and material realism of reference 2. Output ONE finished portrait 9:16 background plate for a game fitting room. No human, android, statue, mannequin, weapon character or UI anywhere. A compact military cybernetic maintenance bay carved inside a SALT MINE, dark rough horizontally layered gray salt, blackened structural steel, practical workshop shelves at far left and right, neatly bundled cables, recessed lockers, understated equipment, one broad cool-white ceiling light above the central bay. Frontal camera 1.2m above ground, natural 42mm lens, very slight downward pitch, coherent one-point perspective. Keep the entire CENTER empty and uncluttered for a live 3D character 1.8m tall; leave broad dark flat DRY concrete/metal floor in lower third so a live rendered round pedestal can be placed on it at about 75 percent image height. DO NOT paint a pedestal or any circular platform into this background: the game renders the real rotating platform. Center rear wall should be calm charcoal gray metal with restrained salt edges, large readable forms, not a noisy rock pattern behind the character. Convey a lived-in expensive practical military sci-fi workshop, industrial engineering not space-opera. No neon strips or glowing colored outlines, no bright blue lighting. Preserve fine physically credible textures, deep readable shadows, photographic dynamic range, subtle practical whites. Clean high quality image without sensor noise, grain, sharpening halos. No water, wet floor, puddles or drips. No text, logos, symbols, frame, interface, characters or pedestal.

Иконки выполнены в нативном SVG, без генерации растровых замен: единые контуры, масштабируемая детализация, наследование цвета состояния.
